# Work Camera — Manual Submission Validation

Revision: 2026-10-02. All scenarios below are **Pending**. Source/syntax checks do not establish Build, Test, or device success. Perform these checks manually with Xcode and physical iPhones. Use disposable captures and a backed-up test device for failure scenarios; do not erase personal media or intentionally exhaust storage on a daily-use phone.

Record for each run: app/build version, device, iOS version, lens, zoom, lighting, permissions, steps, observed result, saved-file Info/metadata, and relevant Xcode console or crash/Jetsam evidence. Mark Pass/Fail/Blocked; missing suitable hardware or an inability to induce a failure is Blocked, not Pass.
