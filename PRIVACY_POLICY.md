# Work Camera Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Work Camera is provided by Sunny Yu. For privacy questions, contact [sonicman212@yahoo.com.hk](mailto:sonicman212@yahoo.com.hk).

## Information stored by the app

Work Camera stores your captured photos and videos, reports, captions, keywords, albums, report templates, and preferences in the app's local storage. Captures may include recording dates, device and camera details, and location metadata when you grant location permission.

Photo search uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to recognize text and classify image content. Recognized text and labels are stored in a local search cache.

We do not operate a server that receives your media library or search results. The app does not require an account and includes no advertising or tracking SDK.

## Permissions

Camera access enables photo and video capture. Microphone access enables audio recording with videos. Optional location access allows capture locations to be saved. Photos add-only access is requested when you choose to save a capture to your system photo library.

You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. Refusing optional permissions may limit the corresponding feature.

## Apple services, sharing, and backups

Maps and address lookups use Apple services and may send saved capture coordinates to Apple to provide those features. Apple's handling of that information is governed by its privacy policy.

When you choose to share, iOS and the receiving app or service handle the selected media and accompanying report. Shared videos and original files saved to Photos may retain location and other metadata.

Files saved to Photos may sync through iCloud Photos according to your settings. App data may also be included in device or iCloud backups. We do not control those services or the data practices of receiving apps.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit or delete them or remove the app and its data. Deleting a capture attempts to remove its original file, related report and information files, and associated search-cache records. Storage or filesystem failures can prevent complete cleanup.

There is no in-app trash or restore feature. Deleting records in Work Camera does not delete copies already saved to Photos, shared with another service, or retained in backups. Manage those copies separately.

## Security

Work Camera uses the iOS app sandbox and system storage protections. It does not implement a separate encryption system for its local files. Protect your device and review your backup and sharing settings.

## Children

Work Camera is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.

## Contact and policy changes

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive work records unless necessary.

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
